AtomQuest Architecture (MVP)

  |  JWT (Bearer)
  v
[Express API]
  |  Prisma Client
  v
[SQLite DB]

- Users (Employee, Manager, Admin)
- GoalSheets -> Goals -> Achievements -> CheckIns
- Cycle (Quarter windows)
- AuditLog (post-lock changes)

Reporting:
- CSV export (planned vs actual)
- Completion dashboard
